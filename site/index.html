Prove Where

Your Coins Were

SHIELDED BEFORE IT HAPPENED.

Choose your Alibi surface.

Prove that shielded ZEC predates an incident without showing a balance, a history or an address, from a wallet, a browser verifier or a command line.

Wallet

Keeps your oldest notes untouched for everyday payments and builds alibi spends against the last shared checkpoint before an incident.

Verifier

Paste any Zcash transaction. Read the snapshot it commits to and the block every one of its inputs provably predates.

CLI

Build, broadcast and check alibi transactions from a terminal, against testnet first and mainnet when ready.

BUILT ON THE ZCASH STACK

KEEP YOUR ALIBI.

Keep your oldest coins old.

Every payment creates new notes dated to the block they land in. Alibi spends your newest notes first, so the ones that predate an incident are still there on the day an exchange or a swap service asks.

Age-aware coin selection.

Everyday payments draw from the youngest notes. Older notes are only touched when you ask for an alibi.

One-tap alibi send.

Pick the incident date. Alibi selects notes older than it, anchors the spend to the shared checkpoint before it and shows exactly what becomes public before you confirm.

NO NEW CRYPTOGRAPHY.

The proof was in every transaction.

Every shielded spend names a past snapshot of the pool, and its zero-knowledge proof forces each spent coin to be inside that snapshot. Consensus accepts any earlier snapshot. Wallets always pick one from a few blocks ago. Alibi picks the one from before the incident.

No viewing key, no history and no addresses leave the wallet.

Anyone can check an alibi from the public transaction alone.

A forged snapshot fails the proof, and the network rejects it.

Explore what makes an alibi hold.

Unforgeable

A spend against a snapshot needs every input inside it. A coin received after the incident cannot produce the proof.

Your keys, your coins

Alibi runs inside your wallet. Nothing is uploaded, and the verifier needs only the public transaction.

Shared checkpoints

Every alibi for one incident uses the same anchor height, so the anchor marks a group of users, not a person.

No blacklist

The verifier reports the block your coins predate. The checker applies its own incident dates. Nobody has to be accused.

Age cannot be bought

Payments always create new notes, so stolen coins cannot acquire an older date by being paid to someone.

Nothing proves more than the protocol itself.

Alibi adds no new trust. It relies on the anchor rule in the Zcash specification and the network's own consensus check, and shows you where each guarantee lives.

Built to be attacked.

A thief anchors a new coin to an old snapshot. The coin is not in that snapshot, so the proof cannot be built, and a forged one fails verification.

Backdated snapshot

Rejected by consensus

Stolen coins are paid to an honest holder in exchange for older ones. The payment creates new notes dated to the block they land in.

Buying age

No date transfers

One old coin and one new coin go into a single spend. A transaction carries one snapshot, so the new coin forces a recent one.

Mixed inputs

Builder refuses

Change from an alibi spend is a new note. Alibi selects exact amounts where it can and reports any change before you confirm.

Change outputs

Disclosed upfront

Observers learn the coins are older than block H, which is the claim itself. The shared checkpoint keeps the anchor from identifying you.

Anchor fingerprint

Cohort anchor

Merkle paths are rebuilt from compact blocks the wallet already downloads. The server sees no request tied to the alibi.

Server observation

No new query

The verifier names no suspects. It returns the block a transaction's inputs predate and leaves incident dates to the checker.

Accusation risk

No list required

Funds received after the incident cannot prove an alibi yet. Alibi says so plainly instead of producing a weaker claim.

Late receipts

Stated plainly

A transaction can spend from Sapling, Orchard and Ironwood at once. Each pool carries its own anchor; Alibi locates every one and the latest decides.

Several pools

Latest anchor decides

Consensus accepts any earlier treestate. Relay policy for old anchors is checked on testnet before any mainnet broadcast.
